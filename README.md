Code to simulate open- and closed-system surface volcanic fluxes for CO2 and S during magma ascent and eruption assuming Wadge-curve effusion rates, particularly for the Auckland Volcanic Field eruptions in Aotearoa New Zealand.

Part of:
Smid, E.R., Hughes, E.C., Kilgour, G. et al. Estimating eruptive volatile emissions and fluxes in the Auckland Volcanic Field, Aotearoa New Zealand. J Appl. Volcanol. 15, 4 (2026). https://doi.org/10.1186/s13617-026-00162-x
